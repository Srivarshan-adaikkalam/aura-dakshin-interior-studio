About Aura & Dakshin

Spaces Born from
Pure Intention

Co-founded by Principal Designer Meera Nair and Lead Architect K. Sundaram in 2012, Aura & Dakshin Studio has grown into South India's premier luxury design firm.

Our philosophy: every sanctuary must be a living dialogue between contemporary ergonomics and regional craftsmanship — celebrating Athangudi tilemaking, teak joinery, and biophilic courtyards.
